# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday people who want to track changing prices, ticket availability, product stock, applications, and other useful updates without repeatedly checking webpages themselves.

## Product Purpose

Notifyr turns a public webpage and a plain-language condition into a dependable background monitor. Success means users can set a monitor quickly, understand its current state at a glance, and receive one useful notification when the condition becomes true.

## Positioning

Tell Notifyr what matters; it quietly watches the web until it is worth interrupting you.

## Brand Personality

Fun, lively, and reassuring. The product should feel optimistic and human without becoming childish or distracting from important monitor state.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic enterprise analytics dashboards, dense infrastructure consoles, fake activity metrics, glass-heavy SaaS styling, and playful decoration that makes monitoring feel unreliable.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action unmistakable.
- Turn technical monitoring states into friendly, plain-language signals.
- Show real user state rather than fabricated dashboard data.
- Use delight to reinforce progress and success, not as decoration.
- Keep privacy and trust visible through calm, predictable interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus treatment, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable on narrow mobile screens.
